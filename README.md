# Jake Estrada

Software engineer and founder of Liminal Innovations & Technology, focused on backend systems, business software, and practical automation.

I graduated with a B.S. in Computer Science in May 2026. I started Liminal Innovations & Technology in May 2025 to build real production experience while solving operational problems I understood firsthand. Since then, I have designed, built, deployed, and maintained software used in day-to-day business operations.

My main project is **Liminnality**, an open-source ERP platform for small businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools. It is designed to be free, self-hostable, and adaptable to the way a business already works, without SaaS lock-in or recurring subscription fees.
